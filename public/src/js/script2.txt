Do any of you guys also struggle with virtual homework?

Hi r/Teachers! I need your help.

**My backstory:**
During the pandemic, I asked other teachers what they were doing for virtual homework. 

Managing Zoom/Google Classroom/Blackboard was already difficult enough for all of them, but then how did they also manage the homework?

One teacher told me that their workflow was to create a Microsoft Word document, they'd spend a while formatting it correctly, and then they would email it out to 300+ students. 

They would then have to make sure that the 300+ students had access to Microsoft Office at home, made sure they could open it and everything worked, and then wait to get submissions back.

They told me that they would usually get emailed back around 300+ word files every other night, and it would take them 3-4 hours to open each Word document individually, write emails with notes on their homework, and then enter their grades into the computers.

Not to mention the sorting of different files, misnamed file names, and students that didn't put their names on papers or filenames.

When I heard about this, I was shocked and thought: "There has to be a better way!"

But everything I found was just file based.

Blackboard and Canvas were extremely overcomplicated and worked best with Microsoft Word files.

Google Drive wasn't an option, because sharing & permissions was too complex.

Even Google Forms and other form websites weren't great solutions, because they were too basic or weren't designed to solve this specific problem.

**Since then:**
I heard that story one year ago, and decided to create a better solution.

I put all hobbies and distractions aside, and I've spent the last year coding and developing a web app called SheetParrot that simplifies this problem.

The idea is that it's all browser based. So no more downloads, Microsoft Office, or word files. A student could fill out the homework on their phone in a matter of minutes.

The pricing is set to have a free plan, and a $15 per month plan.

I tried my best to keep the cost as low as possible to help make it affordable for teachers.

**My Ask:**
It's been a long year just coding away and I haven't gotten feedback in a couple of months.

I actually just finished developing the app a week ago, and I'm now looking for beta users to smooth out all the bugs.

I would love to hear feedback about what you guys are doing today and if you guys think this is a good idea?
